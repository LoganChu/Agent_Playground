Where does a retirement actually get taxed?

Enter what a household receives — not what a tax form says — and this works out the federal return and then all 28 states at once, plus every Maryland and Indiana county. Nothing is sent anywhere: the whole calculation runs in this page.

What this is

A front end for two open-source, dependency-free tax engines — us-federal-tax and us-state-tax — compiled straight to ES modules and run in your browser. There is no server and no analytics; close the tab and nothing remains. The same engines are available as an MCP server so a language model can call them instead of guessing.

Source, tests and provenance on GitHub. Every published figure is cited to the Revenue Procedure or state release it came from, and every carried-forward figure is flagged as provisional.

Not tax advice. An estimate of income tax only, for a full-year resident of one state, and it leaves things out — read the What is missing notes under each state. Check anything that matters against the forms or a professional.
